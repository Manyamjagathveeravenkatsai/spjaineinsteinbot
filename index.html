About SPJIMR

Welcome to S. P. Jain Institute of Management & Research, a leading management institution located in Mumbai, India. SPJIMR focuses on academic excellence, industry engagement, social sensitivity and value-based growth.

Our purpose is to develop responsible leaders who create meaningful impact in business and society through innovative learning, research and real-world experiences.
